Privacy Policy — Vault Ledger

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Vault Ledger is published by 2351485 ALBERTA INC. Questions about this policy or the app: info@aimlds.org.

The short version

Vault Ledger collects nothing, shares nothing, and has no server to send your data to. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter in Vault Ledger — your expense and income records, amounts, descriptions and classifications — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local database (IndexedDB). Your content is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is stored; the encryption key is derived from the master password you choose (PBKDF2) and never leaves your device. We never see your password, your keys, or your data, and we could not read your data if we wanted to: there is no backend, no cloud sync operated by us, and the app makes no network calls with your content.

Data we collect

None. 2351485 ALBERTA INC. receives no data from Vault Ledger: no personal information, no usage data, no diagnostics, no crash reports, no device identifiers.

Data sharing

None, with one exception that is entirely in your hands: exports and sharing are user-initiated only. If you choose to export a file (JSON, CSV or text) or use your device's share function, the data goes only where you send it. We are not involved in that transfer and receive no copy.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet permission because the app is built on web technology; it is not used to transmit your content anywhere. The app requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, or files outside the files you explicitly choose to import or export. Optional voice input: if you tap the microphone button and grant microphone permission, speech recognition is provided by your browser / operating system's speech service, which processes the audio under that provider's own privacy policy; the resulting text is parsed on your device and we receive no audio and no transcript. Voice input is off until you tap the button.

Deleting your data

Your data is yours and lives on your device. Deleting a vault inside the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app removes it. Because your password cannot be recovered by us (zero-knowledge design), a lost password means the encrypted data cannot be opened — by you or by anyone else.

Children

Vault Ledger is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. The app's data behaviour described above — nothing collected, nothing shared — is its architecture, not a setting.
